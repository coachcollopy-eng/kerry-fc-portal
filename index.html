HYPERTROPHY NUTRITION

NUTRITION & RECOVERY DIRECTIVES

Fueling guidelines to support getting Bigger, Stronger and More Powerful.

OFF-SEASON DO'S

  • Prioritize Protein Intake: Consume 1.8g – 2.2g of protein per kg of body weight daily.
  • Carbohydrate Timing: Eat quality carbs before and immediately after sessions.
  • Hydration Standards: Drink 3.5L to 4L of water daily.

OFF-SEASON DON'TS

  • DON'T Skip Meals: Maintain positive energy balance.
  • DON'T Rely on Junk Calories: Avoid ultra-processed foods.
  • DON'T Excessively Consume Alcohol: Disrupts muscle protein synthesis.
